supabackup integrates with dead-man switches such as healthchecks.io: after
every successful AND fresh backup it pings your URL; production-path failures
ping `<url>/fail`.

## Configuration

In the database's "Schedule & heartbeat" drawer:

- **Heartbeat URL**: empty inherits the server-wide `SB_HEARTBEAT_URL`;
  `-` disables explicitly (no success and no fail pings).
- **Expected period (hours)**: required when a URL is set. Your external
  monitor alerts when pings stop for period+grace.
- **Grace (hours)**: extra tolerance on the period.

## When a success ping fires

1. an effective URL exists (per-database or fallback) and is not `-`;
2. the expected period is > 0;
3. the time from THIS run's export start (dumpStart) to now is within
   period + grace — not the completion time, and not a poll of history: a
   very long successful run may legitimately not ping;
4. with a remote destination configured: that backup must be remote-committed
   (local-only deployments are exempt).

Fail pings bypass the age gate (the failure IS the signal) but still respect
`-`. Your external monitor's alerting period is configured on the monitor
side; supabackup only pings.

<Callout>
One URL expresses "this monitor is alive". With several databases sharing the
fallback URL, any healthy one refreshes it — configure per-database URLs when
you need per-database monitoring.
</Callout>
